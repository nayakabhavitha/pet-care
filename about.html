About Bhavitha PawCare

🐶 Caring for Pets, Creating Healthy Lives

Welcome to Bhavitha PawCare, a friendly veterinary clinic dedicated to providing quality healthcare for pets.

Our clinic provides professional veterinary care, regular health checkups, vaccinations, treatments, grooming guidance and emergency support for your beloved pets.

We believe every pet deserves love, care and a healthy life. Our goal is to make every visit comfortable for both pets and their owners.
